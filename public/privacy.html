Privacy: the "Ask about my experience" assistant

Last updated 21 October 2026

This page covers the assistant only. The rest of the site has no accounts, no cookies and no tracking.

What's stored, and why

Each question you ask, the assistant's answer, and how the request went (when it arrived, how long it took, and how many tokens it used) are stored so I can find wrong answers and improve the assistant. Earlier questions in the same conversation are sent along for context, but only their number is stored with the next one. Please don't include personal information in your questions.

Only I (Jack) can read the stored questions. They're deleted automatically after 90 days.

IP addresses

IP addresses aren't stored. They're held in memory only for rate limiting, which stops one visitor using up the assistant, and forgotten at midnight UTC.

Who processes your questions

Answers are generated by Anthropic's Claude, an AI model hosted on Microsoft Azure through Microsoft Foundry. Anthropic processes your questions and the answers to generate replies, and its staff may review content that its automatic safeguards flag, to investigate possible safety violations. Microsoft provides the Azure hosting and billing. Your questions may be processed in any Azure region. The assistant itself runs as an Azure Function in the United States, and its error and timing logs don't include your questions.

Why, and your rights

I store questions on the basis of my legitimate interest in finding wrong answers and keeping the assistant from being abused. You can ask to see the questions you sent, have them deleted, or object to their being stored: email jack.treadwell.dev@gmail.com with roughly when you asked and what, and I'll act on it. If you have further concerns, feel free to reach out to the relevant civil authority.

Accuracy

The assistant answers only from this site and can be wrong. Check the resume or email me to confirm anything important.
